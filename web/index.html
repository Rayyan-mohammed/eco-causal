A biodiversity advisor that checks its own reasoning
before it answers you.

Standard RAG chatbots verify that a fact exists in a source. ROOTCAUSE goes further: every multi-variable recommendation is extracted into a causal chain and checked, edge by edge, against a source-cited map — before it ever reaches you.
